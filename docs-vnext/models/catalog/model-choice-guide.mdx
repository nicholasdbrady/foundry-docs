Choose between GPT-5 and GPT-4.1 based on reasoning requirements, latency, throughput, and cost. GPT-5 supports four adjustable reasoning levels and is suited to complex analysis, coding, and agentic workflows. GPT-4.1 prioritizes fast, predictable responses for real-time and high-volume applications.

## GPT-5 vs GPT-4.1 comparison

| Feature | GPT-5 | GPT-4.1 |
|---|---|---|
| Model type | Reasoning | Non-reasoning |
| Best for | Complex reasoning, multi-hop logic, planning, and tool use | Real-time chat, short factual queries, and high-throughput workloads |
| Latency | Higher because of reasoning and longer outputs | Lower and optimized for responsiveness |
| Throughput | Moderate | High |
| Token length | 272K input and 128K output tokens (400K total) | 128K short context or up to 1M long context |
| Response style | Structured, analytical, and step-by-step | Concise, fast, and conversational |
| Cost | [Pricing](https://azure.microsoft.com/pricing/details/cognitive-services/openai-service/) | [Pricing](https://azure.microsoft.com/pricing/details/cognitive-services/openai-service/) |
| Variants | GPT-5, GPT-5-mini, GPT-5-nano | GPT-4.1, GPT-4.1-mini, GPT-4.1-nano |

## GPT-5 thinking levels trade-offs

| Reasoning effort | Depth | Latency and cost | Reliability | Typical uses |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Minimal** | Very shallow; few or no reasoning tokens | Fastest and lowest | Lowest on complex tasks | Bulk operations and simple transformations |
| **Low** | Shallow to light | Fast and low | Moderate | Triage, short answers, and simple edits |
| **Medium (default)** | Moderate | Moderate | Suitable for most tasks | Content drafting, moderate coding, and RAG Q&A |
| **High** | Deep, multistep reasoning | Slowest and highest | Highest | Complex planning, analysis, and multi-hop reasoning |

These trade-offs apply to GPT-5, GPT-5-mini, and GPT-5-nano, although the mini and nano variants have lower absolute latency and cost. Minimal reasoning does not support parallel tool calls; use Low, Medium, or High when parallel tool use is required.

## Choose a model by workload

| Requirement or workload | Recommended model | Guidance |
|---|---|---|
| Research reports, legal or financial analysis, and complex synthesis | GPT-5 with Medium or High reasoning | Prioritize reasoning quality when longer response times are acceptable. |
| Coding and technical troubleshooting assistants | GPT-5 or GPT-5-mini | Adjust reasoning effort to match task complexity. |
| Agentic or enterprise Copilot workflows | GPT-5 | Use its planning and tool-calling capabilities for multi-step tasks. Use structured outputs for predictable formats and verbosity controls for response length. |
| Customer support, product recommendations, and user-facing chat | GPT-4.1 | Prioritize low latency and concise, conversational responses. |
| High-volume Q&A, search, or lightweight summarization | GPT-4.1 | Use its high throughput and cost efficiency. |
| Long-context processing | GPT-4.1 long-context | Supports inputs up to 1M tokens. |

If requirements vary by request, use [Model Router](https://ai.azure.com/catalog/models/model-router). It routes across GPT-5 family and other Foundry models and can reduce inference cost by up to 60% while maintaining comparable quality. For configuration instructions, see [Use model router for Foundry](https://learn.microsoft.com/azure/ai-foundry/openai/how-to/model-router).

## Latency considerations

GPT-5 takes longer to begin and produce responses because it spends more time reasoning. GPT-4.1 is more responsive for real-time interactions and short prompts.

| Metric | GPT-5 | GPT-4.1 |
|---|---|---|
| Time to first token (TTFT) | Higher | Lower |
| Time between tokens (TBT) | Moderate to high | Low |
| User experience | May feel slower, especially for short prompts | Feels fast and responsive |

For GPT-5 workloads that require predictable latency, use [Provisioned Throughput](https://learn.microsoft.com/azure/ai-foundry/openai/concepts/provisioned-throughput), which provides latency service-level agreements. See [Get started with Provisioned Throughput](https://learn.microsoft.com/azure/ai-foundry/openai/how-to/provisioned-get-started).
